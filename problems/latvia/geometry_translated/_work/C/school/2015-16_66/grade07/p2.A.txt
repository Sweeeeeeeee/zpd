Take a segment OA. C is the reflection of A through O. B is an arbitrary point on the circle centered at O through A. X is the point on line AB lying on the bisector of angle AOB (at O). Y is the point on line BC lying on the bisector of angle BOC (at O). (Bisector construction is by directed angles, so internal or external bisector line is possible.) Prove that OX is perpendicular to OY.
