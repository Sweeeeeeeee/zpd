Free points: O and A, arbitrary. Let omega be the circle with center O through A.

Construction:
- B: any point on omega.
- C: intersection of the tangent to omega at A (the line through A perpendicular to OA) with the tangent to omega at B (the line through B perpendicular to OB). These meet at C, which requires B not diametrically opposite A.
- P: any point on omega (one free parameter).
- D: foot of the perpendicular from P to line AB.
- E: foot of the perpendicular from P to line AC.
- F: foot of the perpendicular from P to line BC.

Goal: PD / PE = PF / PD, i.e. PD squared = PE times PF.
