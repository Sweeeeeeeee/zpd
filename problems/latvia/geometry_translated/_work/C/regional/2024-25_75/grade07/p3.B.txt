Take a segment BC and let U be any point on the circle with center B through C. Let U2 be the reflection of C over line BU. Let U3 be the reflection of U over line BU2, U4 the reflection of U2 over line BU3, U5 the reflection of U3 over line BU4, U6 the reflection of U4 over line BU5 (successive reflections, all on that circle). Let M be the point on line BU5 with directed angle (MB, MC) = directed angle (BC, BU5). Let N be the point with directed angle (CN, CB) = directed angle (BC, BU6) and directed angle (NB, NC) = directed angle (BC, BU4). Let A be the intersection of lines BN and CM. Prove that directed angle (BA, BC) = directed angle (CB, CA).
