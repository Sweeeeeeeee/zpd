Same statement as x07 (C and A are two arbitrary points, as in x07).

Free points: C and A, arbitrary.

Construction:
- B: a point on the line through C such that the directed angle from line CA to line CB is 45 degrees (counterclockwise). This fixes only the line CB, so B is any point of that line other than C. The undirected angle ACB is 45 degrees or 135 degrees, depending on the side of C on which B lies.
- M: foot of the perpendicular from A to line BC.
- N: foot of the perpendicular from B to line CA.
- S: midpoint of AB.

Goal: SM = SN and SM is perpendicular to SN.
