Take any circle with centre O and a point A on it (O, A free). Choose B, C, D on the circle (any positions, in the order giving a quadrilateral). Let P = AB ∩ CD and Q = AD ∩ BC. Let W be the circumcentre of triangle APQ. Let R be the second intersection (other than A) of the circle centre O through A and the circle centre W through A. Let K be the midpoint of PQ. Prove that C, R, K are collinear.
