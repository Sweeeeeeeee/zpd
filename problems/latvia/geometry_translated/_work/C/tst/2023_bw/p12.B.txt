Free points: B and C, arbitrary (two points).

Construction:
- M: midpoint of BC.
- T: any point on the circle with center M through B (one free parameter), so MT = MB.
- F: intersection of the line through T perpendicular to MT with the line through B parallel to MT.
- B2: reflection of B in point F (F is the midpoint of BB2).
- A: intersection of the line through T perpendicular to MT with the line CB2.
- K: intersection of line BC with the line through A parallel to MT.
- L: intersection of line AK with the circumcircle of ABC, other than A.
- O1: circumcenter of triangle KML.
- M2: reflection of M in O1 (O1 is the midpoint of MM2).

Goal: MM2 = MB.
