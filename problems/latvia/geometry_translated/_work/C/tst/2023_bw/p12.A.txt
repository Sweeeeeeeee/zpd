Free points: B and C, arbitrary (two points).

Construction:
- M: midpoint of BC.
- T: any point on the circle with center M through B (one free parameter).
- U: a point with UT perpendicular to TM and UT = TM. The sketch takes the one obtained by rotating the vector TM by 90 degrees about T; the other choice is the mirror image of U in line TM. U is a vertex of the square with side TM, adjacent to T.
- W: reflection of B in line TU.
- A: intersection of line TU with line CW.
- K: intersection of line BC with the line through A perpendicular to TU.
- L: intersection of line AK with the circumcircle of ABC, other than A.
- O1: circumcenter of triangle KML.
- X: reflection of M in O1 (O1 is the midpoint of MX).

Goal (two length equalities):
- MX = MB
- O1X = O1K
