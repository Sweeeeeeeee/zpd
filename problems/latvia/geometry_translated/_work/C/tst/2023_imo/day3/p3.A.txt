Let ABC be any triangle and F the foot of the altitude from A to BC. Let P be any point on line AF. Let D be the point on line BC such that PD is parallel to AC, and E the point on line BC such that PE is parallel to AB. Let U be the circumcentre of triangle ABD and V the circumcentre of triangle ACE. Let X be the reflection of A over line DU, and Y the reflection of A over line EV. Prove that B, C, X, Y are concyclic.
