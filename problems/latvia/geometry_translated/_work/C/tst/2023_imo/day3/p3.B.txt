Let ABC be any triangle and F the foot of the altitude from A to BC. Let P be any point on line AF. Let D be the point on line BC such that PD is parallel to AC, and E the point on line BC such that PE is parallel to AB. Let O1 be the circumcentre of triangle ABD and X the reflection of A over line DO1. Let O2 be the circumcentre of triangle ACE and Y the reflection of A over line EO2. Prove that B, C, X, Y are concyclic.
