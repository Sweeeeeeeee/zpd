Let ABCD be an isosceles trapezoid with bases BC and AD parallel (AD ∥ BC) and equal legs AB = CD (otherwise arbitrary). Let I be the incentre of triangle ABC and X the intersection of line AI with line BC. Let J be the incentre of triangle BCD. Let E be the point on line BD such that XE is parallel to BJ, and F the point on line DC such that XF is parallel to CJ. Prove that A, E, F, D are concyclic.
