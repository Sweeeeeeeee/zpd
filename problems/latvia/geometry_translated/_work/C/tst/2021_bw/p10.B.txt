Take two free points M and X. Let Y be the reflection of M through X (Y = 2X - M; so Y is on line MX beyond X, at distance MX from X). Let A be any point on the circle with centre M through X. Let B be any point on line XM; C = reflection of B through M. A1 = reflection of A through M (antipode of A). P = second intersection (other than A1) of line CA1 with the circle centre M through A1. Q = second intersection (other than A1) of line BA1 with that circle. S = intersection of lines PQ and XY (XY is the line XM). Prove that AS is perpendicular to AM.
