Take any circle with centre O through a free point B (O and B are free). Choose three more points C, E, A on the circle (any positions). Let M be the midpoint of chord BE. Let D be the reflection of C over the line OM. Let F be the intersection of the tangent to the circle at B (line through B perpendicular to OB) with line AC. Let G be the intersection of lines BD and AE. Let Q be the circumcentre of triangle ADG. Prove that FG is perpendicular to QG (FG is tangent to circle ADG at G).
