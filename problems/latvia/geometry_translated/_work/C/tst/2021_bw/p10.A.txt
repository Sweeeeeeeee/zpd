Take two free points M and X. Let Y be the reflection of X through M (Y = 2M - X), so XY is a diameter of the circle centre M through X. Let A be any point on that circle (centre M, through X). Let B be any point on line XM; C = reflection of B through M. Z = reflection of A through M (antipode of A on the circle). P = second intersection (other than Z) of line CZ with the circle centre M through Z. Q = second intersection (other than Z) of line BZ with the same circle. S = intersection of lines PQ and XY (note XY is the line XM, which also contains B and C). Prove that AS is perpendicular to AM.
