Take any circle with centre O, and a point A on it (O and A are free). Choose three more points B, C, E on the circle (any positions). Let M be the midpoint of chord BE. Let D be the reflection of C over the line OM (line through the centre and M). Let F be the intersection of the tangent to the circle at B (line through B perpendicular to OB) with line AC. Let G be the intersection of lines BD and AE. Let Q be the circumcentre of triangle ADG. Prove that FG is perpendicular to GQ (i.e. FG is tangent to circle ADG at G).
