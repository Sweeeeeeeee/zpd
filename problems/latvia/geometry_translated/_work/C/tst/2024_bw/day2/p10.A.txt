Let ABC be an isosceles triangle with AB = AC (apex A). Let F be the foot of the perpendicular from B to AC. H = second intersection (other than B) of line FB with the circle centre A through B. Let K be the midpoint of AC. G = second intersection (other than B) of line KB with the circle centre A through B. Let X be the intersection of line GH and line AC. Prove that C is the midpoint of AX.
