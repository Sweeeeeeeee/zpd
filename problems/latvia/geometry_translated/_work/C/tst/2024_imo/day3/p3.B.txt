Free points: A, B, C, forming an arbitrary triangle (non-degenerate).

Construction:
- O: circumcenter of ABC.
- I: incenter of ABC.
- P: reflection of I in line BC.
- S: foot of the perpendicular from P to line AB. (S is not used by any later point or by the goal.)
- D: intersection of line BP with the circumcircle of ABC, other than B.
- Y: intersection of line CI with the circumcircle of ABC, other than C.
- Q: the point with Q = P + O - Y, so that Q P Y O is a parallelogram (QP parallel and equal to OY).
- E: intersection of the line through A perpendicular to CQ with the line through B perpendicular to DQ.

Goal: A, B, C, E lie on one circle (cyclic A B C E).
