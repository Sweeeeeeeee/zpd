Let ABC be any triangle. Choose arbitrary points D on line BC, E on line AC, F on line AB. Let K be the point such that (directed angles between lines) angle(FK,FE) = angle(DB,DE) and angle(KA,KF) = angle(BA,BC) (K lies on a line through F and on the circle-arc locus over chord AF; a unique point). Let L be the point such that angle(EL,EF) = angle(DC,DF) and angle(LA,LE) = angle(CA,CB). Prove that angle(KA,KB) = angle(LA,LC) (directed angles between lines).
