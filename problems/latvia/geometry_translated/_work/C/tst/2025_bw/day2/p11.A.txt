Take two free points O and B. Let M be the third vertex of an equilateral triangle OBM (either orientation). Let C be the reflection of B over line OM. Let Z be the reflection of M through O (Z = 2O - M). So B, C, M, Z all lie on the circle with centre O through B (radius OB). Let V be any point on the circle with diameter BC and T the foot of the perpendicular from V to BC (so T is an arbitrary point of segment BC). Let A be the second intersection (other than Z) of line TZ with the circle centre O through Z. Let P be the intersection of the tangents to the circle at A and at B (lines through A perp. to OA, through B perp. to OB). Let Q be the intersection of the tangents at A and at C. In triangle OPQ let H be the orthocentre and I the incentre. N = midpoint of OI. D = second intersection (other than M) of line NM with the circle centre O through M. Prove that AD is perpendicular to HI.
