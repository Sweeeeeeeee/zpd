M and B are two arbitrary distinct points. A is the reflection of B through M (M is the midpoint of AB). N is the point on the line through B perpendicular to BM such that the directed angle from line MB to line MN is 30 degrees. N2 is the reflection of N over line AB. D is the point on the line through A perpendicular to AB that also lies on the line through N parallel to M N2. C = B + D - A (so that A, B, C, D form a parallelogram with vertices in order A, B, C, D). Prove that MC = CD and MD = CD (triangle MCD is equilateral).
