A and B are two arbitrary distinct points; M is the midpoint of AB. N is the point on the line through B perpendicular to AB such that the directed angle from line MB to line MN is 30 degrees. Q is the point such that ABNQ is a rectangle: Q lies on the line through N parallel to AB and on the line through A perpendicular to AB. D is the point on the line through A perpendicular to AB such that the directed angle from line NQ to line ND is 150 degrees (mod 180). C = D + B - A, so that ABCD is a parallelogram (vertices in order A, B, C, D). Prove that CD = DM and DM = MC.
