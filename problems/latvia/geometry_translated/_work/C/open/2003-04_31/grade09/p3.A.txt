A and B are two arbitrary distinct points. C is obtained by rotating A by 90 degrees (counterclockwise) about B, so BC is perpendicular to BA and BC = BA. D = A + C - B, so ABCD is a square (vertices in order A, B, C, D). O is an arbitrary point on the perpendicular bisector of AB. E is the second intersection of line DB with the circle centered O through B (the circle passes through A and B). F is the second intersection of line CB with the same circle. M is the midpoint of CF. Prove that EM is perpendicular to BC.
