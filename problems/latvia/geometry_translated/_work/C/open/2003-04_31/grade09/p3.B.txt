A and B are two arbitrary distinct points. C is obtained by rotating A about B by 90 degrees clockwise (BC perpendicular to BA, BC = BA). D is obtained by rotating B about A by 90 degrees counterclockwise (AD perpendicular to AB, AD = AB); C and D lie on the same side of AB, so ABCD is a square with vertices in order. O is an arbitrary point on the perpendicular bisector of AB. E is a point on line BD and on the circle with center O through A (this circle also passes through B, so E is either B or the second intersection; two possible points). F is a point on line BC and on the circle with center O through B (again either B or the second intersection). M is the midpoint of CF. Prove that EM is perpendicular to BC.
