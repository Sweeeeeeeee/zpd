O, A, B1 are three arbitrary points (any triangle). K1 is the point on the line through A perpendicular to OA that is equidistant from A and B1 (center of the circle through A and B1 tangent to OA at A). C1 is a point on line OB1 and on that circle (the circle centered K1 through A; line OB1 meets it in B1 and one more point, so C1 may be either). I1 is the incenter of triangle A B1 C1. B2 is an arbitrary point on line OB1; K2 is built from A and B2 as K1 was from A and B1 (on the perpendicular to OA at A, equidistant from A and B2); C2 is a point on line OB1 (= line OB2) and on the circle centered K2 through A (two possible points); I2 is the incenter of A B2 C2. B3 is another arbitrary point on line OB1 with K3, C3, I3 built likewise. Prove that I1, I2, I3 are collinear.
