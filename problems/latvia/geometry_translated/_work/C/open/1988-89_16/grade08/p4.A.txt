Let O, A, B1 be an arbitrary triangle. W1 is the point on the line through A perpendicular to OA that is equidistant from A and B1 (the center of the circle through A and B1 tangent to OA at A). C1 is the second intersection of line OB1 with the circle centered W1 through B1 (the circle through A and B1 tangent to OA at A). I1 is the incenter of triangle A B1 C1. Then B2 is an arbitrary point strictly between O and B1; W2, C2, I2 are built the same way from B2 (W2 on the perpendicular to OA at A and equidistant from A and B2; C2 the second intersection of line OB2 with the circle centered W2 through B2; I2 the incenter of A B2 C2). B3 is another arbitrary point strictly between O and B1, with W3, C3, I3 built the same way. Prove that I1, I2, I3 are collinear.
