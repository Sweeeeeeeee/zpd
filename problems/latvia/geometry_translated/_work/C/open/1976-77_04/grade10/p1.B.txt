O1 is an arbitrary point and A another arbitrary point. O2 and O3 are two arbitrary points on the circle with center O1 through A. P is a common point of the circle with center O2 through A and the circle with center O3 through A; Q is a common point of the circle with center O1 through A and the circle with center O3 through A. Each pair of circles meets in two points (A itself and one other), so as literally written P and Q may each be either A or the other point (intended: the other point). Prove that O2, P, Q are collinear.
