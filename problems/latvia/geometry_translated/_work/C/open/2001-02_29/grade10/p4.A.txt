B and M are two arbitrary distinct points. N is a point on the line through B making a directed angle of 60 degrees from line BM (one free parameter: N may be anywhere on that line). E is the excenter of triangle BMN opposite vertex B. A is the reflection of N over line ME. C is the reflection of M over line NE. O is the circumcenter of triangle ABC. Prove that A, N, O are collinear and C, M, O are collinear.
