B and M are two arbitrary distinct points. N is a point on the line through B making a directed angle of 60 degrees from line BM (N may be anywhere on that line). I is the incenter of triangle BMN. A0 is the reflection of N over line MI; A is the reflection of A0 through the point M (M is the midpoint of A0 A). C0 is the reflection of M over line NI; C is the reflection of C0 through the point N (N is the midpoint of C0 C). O is the intersection of lines AN and CM. Prove that OA = OB and OB = OC.
