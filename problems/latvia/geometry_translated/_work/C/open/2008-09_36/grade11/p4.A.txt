ABCD is an arbitrary quadrilateral (four points in general position). K = A + C - D (A shifted by the vector from D to C). F is an arbitrary point on the circle with center A through K. H = F + C - B (F shifted by vector B to C). G = D + A - B (D shifted by vector B to A). E is a common point of the circle with center D through G and the circle with center F through H (two possible positions). Let A1, B1, C1, D1, E1, F1 be the midpoints of AC, BD, CE, DF, EA, FB respectively. X is the intersection of lines A1D1 and B1E1. Prove that X, C1, F1 are collinear.
