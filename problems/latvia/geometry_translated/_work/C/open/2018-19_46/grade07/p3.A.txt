A and D are two arbitrary distinct points. P is an arbitrary point on line AD. B is an arbitrary point on the circle with center A through P. C is an arbitrary point on the circle with center D through P. M is the point on the bisector of angle BAD (vertex A) and on the bisector of angle ADC (vertex D); the bisectors are taken as directed-angle bisectors, so each may be the internal or the external bisector line. Prove that MB = MC.
