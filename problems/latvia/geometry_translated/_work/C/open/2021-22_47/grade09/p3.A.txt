Free points: A, B, C, D, forming an arbitrary quadrilateral ABCD (no convexity assumed).

Construction:
- E: the fourth vertex of the parallelogram ABDE in that order, so AB is parallel to DE, AE is parallel to BD, and E = A + D - B.
- F: intersection of the line through E parallel to BC with the line through A parallel to CD. These lines are not parallel for generic points.

Goal (two length equalities):
- BC = EF
- CD = AF
