Take two arbitrary distinct points A and D. Let P be a point strictly between A and D on segment AD. Let B be a point on the circle with center A through P. Let C be a point on the circle with center D through P that also lies on the circumcircle of triangle ABD (two intersection points are possible; C could be either). Let X be the intersection of the bisector of angle ABC (vertex B) with the bisector of angle BCD (vertex C). Prove that X lies on line AD.
