Free points: A, B, C, forming an arbitrary triangle (non-degenerate).

Construction:
- I: incenter of ABC.
- A1: foot of the perpendicular from I to line BC.
- B1: foot of the perpendicular from I to line CA.
- C1: foot of the perpendicular from I to line AB.
- K: intersection of the line through A parallel to BC with the line A1C1 (the line through A1 and C1).

Goal: line KB1 is perpendicular to line B1A1 (perp K B1 B1 A1).
