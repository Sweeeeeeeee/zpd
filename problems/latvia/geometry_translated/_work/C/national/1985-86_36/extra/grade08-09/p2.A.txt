Take a circle with centre O and a point A on it; B and C are further arbitrary points on the circle (O, A free). Let S be any point in the plane. Let A1, B1, C1 be the second intersections of lines SA, SB, SC with the circle (other than A, B, C respectively). Let A2, B2, C2 be the feet of the perpendiculars from S to lines BC, AC, AB respectively (pedal triangle of S). Prove that triangles A1B1C1 and A2B2C2 are similar, with vertices corresponding in that order.
