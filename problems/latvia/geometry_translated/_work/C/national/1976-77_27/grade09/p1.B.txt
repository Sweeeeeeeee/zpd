Take an arbitrary quadrilateral ABCD. Let the four points be the incenters of triangles DAB, ABC, BCD, CDA respectively (call them I1..I4 in that order). Through A draw the line perpendicular to AI1, through B the line perpendicular to BI2, through C the line perpendicular to CI3, through D the line perpendicular to DI4. Let the four points be the successive intersections: line at A with line at B, line at B with line at C, line at C with line at D, line at D with line at A. Prove that these four points are concyclic.
