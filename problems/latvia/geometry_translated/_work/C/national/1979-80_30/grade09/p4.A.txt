Take three free points C, B, E. Let D be obtained from B by rotating about C by +90 degrees (counterclockwise assumed), so CD = CB and CD ⟂ CB. Let A = B + D - C, so ABCD is a square. Let G be obtained from E by rotating about C by -90 degrees in the opposite sense to D's rotation (CG = CE, CG ⟂ CE, nsquare convention: clockwise), and F = E + G - C, so CEFG is a square. M = midpoint of BE, K = midpoint of DG. Prove that CM is perpendicular to DG and CM = DK.
