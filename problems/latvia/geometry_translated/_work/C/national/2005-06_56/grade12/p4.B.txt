Let ABC be an acute triangle. Let K be the midpoint of AB. Let M be a point on line AC and on the circle with diameter AB (this gives A or the second intersection; the second is intended), and N a point on line BC and on that circle (B or the second intersection; the second is intended). Let O be the intersection of the line through M perpendicular to KM and the line through N perpendicular to KN. Prove that OM = OC and ON = OC.
