Let ABC be an acute triangle. Let W be the midpoint of AB. Let M be the second intersection of line CA with the circle with center W through A (circle with diameter AB), and N the second intersection of line CB with the circle with center W through B. Let O be the intersection of the line through M perpendicular to WM and the line through N perpendicular to WN (the tangents at M and N to that circle). Prove that OM = OC and ON = OC.
