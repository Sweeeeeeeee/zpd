Free points: A, B, C, forming an arbitrary acute triangle (all three angles less than 90 degrees).

Construction:
- A1: foot of the perpendicular from A to line BC.
- B1: foot of the perpendicular from B to line AC.
- H: orthocenter of ABC.
- M: midpoint of AB.
- N: midpoint of AH.
- K: midpoint of BH.

Goal (three length equalities):
- MK = B1N
- KA1 = NM
- A1M = MB1
