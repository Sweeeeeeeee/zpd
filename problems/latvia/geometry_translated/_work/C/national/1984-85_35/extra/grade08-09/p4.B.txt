Take an arbitrary quadrilateral with four free points A, B, C, E. Let A1 be the midpoint of EB and B1 the midpoint of AC. Let O be the intersection of lines AA1 and BB1. Let B2 be the reflection of B through O and B3 the reflection of B through C. Let D1 be the midpoint of CE. Let D be the intersection of line B2B3 and line OD1. Let E1 be the midpoint of DA. Prove that E, E1, O are collinear.
