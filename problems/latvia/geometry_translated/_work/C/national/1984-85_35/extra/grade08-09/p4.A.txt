Take an arbitrary quadrilateral ABCD. Let B1 be the midpoint of AC and C1 the midpoint of BD, and O the intersection of lines BB1 and CC1. Let P be the reflection of B through A, Q the reflection of B through O, R the reflection of C through D, S the reflection of C through O. Let E be the intersection of lines PQ and RS. Let E1 be the midpoint of DA. Prove that E, E1, O are collinear.
