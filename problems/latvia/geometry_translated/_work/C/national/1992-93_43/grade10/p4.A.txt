Let ABC be an acute triangle. Let K be the midpoint of AC and B1 the foot of the altitude from B to AC. Let P be the point on line BB1 that lies on the circle with center K through A (two points, either may be chosen). Let Q be the second intersection of line BP with the circle with center K through P. Let L be the midpoint of AB and C1 the foot of the altitude from C to AB. Let M be a point on line CC1 on the circle with center L through A (two choices). Let N be the second intersection of line CM with the circle with center L through M. Prove that P, Q, M, N are concyclic.
