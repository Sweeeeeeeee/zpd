Let ABC be an acute triangle. Let B1 be the foot of the altitude from B to AC. Let P be a point on line BB1 and on the circle with diameter AC (two choices). Let Q be the reflection of P through B1. Let C1 be the foot of the altitude from C to AB. Let M be a point on line CC1 on the circle with diameter AB (two choices). Let N be the reflection of M through C1. Prove that P, Q, M, N are concyclic.
