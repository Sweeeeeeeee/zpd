Take a segment AB (free) and any point C on line AB (not necessarily between A and B). Construct equilateral triangles: B1 is the apex of the equilateral triangle on AC lying to the left of directed line A->C (counterclockwise convention); A1 is the apex of the equilateral triangle on CB lying to the left of C->B; C1 is the apex of the equilateral triangle on BA lying to the left of B->A (so, for C between A and B, A1 and B1 are on one side of AB and C1 on the other). Let S = AA1 ∩ BB1. Prove that angle(SB1,SA) = angle(AC,AB1) (directed angles between lines) and that S, C, C1 are collinear.
