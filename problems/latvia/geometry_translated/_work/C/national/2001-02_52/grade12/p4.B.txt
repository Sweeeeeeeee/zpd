Let C be a point strictly between A and B (A, B free, C inside segment AB). Construct equilateral triangles: B1 is the apex of the equilateral triangle on AC to the left of directed line A->C (counterclockwise convention); A1 the apex of the equilateral triangle on CB to the left of C->B (same side as B1); C1 the apex of the equilateral triangle on BA to the left of B->A (the opposite side of AB). Let S = AA1 ∩ BB1. Prove that angle(SB1,SA) = angle(AC,AB1) (directed angles between lines) and that S, C, C1 are collinear.
