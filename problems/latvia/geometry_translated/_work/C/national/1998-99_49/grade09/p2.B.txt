ABC is an equilateral triangle (either orientation). M is an arbitrary point on line AB. N is a point on the circle with center M through C such that the directed angle (CN, CM) equals the directed angle (CB, CA) (angles between lines, mod 180 degrees). The line through C with that direction meets the circle in C itself and one more point, so N is effectively determined, though the construction may also allow N = C. Prove that BN is parallel to AC.
