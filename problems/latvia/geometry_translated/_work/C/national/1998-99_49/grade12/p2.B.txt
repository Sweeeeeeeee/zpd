Take three arbitrary points O, A and O2. Let B be the reflection of A over the line O O2. Let M be any point on line AB. Let X be a point on the circle with center O through A such that MX is perpendicular to OM (two choices). Let Y be the reflection of X through M. Let T be a point on the circle with center O2 through A such that MT is perpendicular to O2M (two choices), and Z the reflection of T through M. Prove that XY = TZ and that XT is perpendicular to XZ.
