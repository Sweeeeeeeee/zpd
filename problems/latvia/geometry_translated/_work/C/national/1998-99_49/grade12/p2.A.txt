Take three arbitrary points O1, O2 and A. Let B be the reflection of A over the line O1O2. Let M be any point on line AB. Let X be a point on the circle with center O1 through A such that MX is perpendicular to O1M (two such points exist; either may be chosen). Let Y be the second intersection of line MX with that circle. Let T be a point on the circle with center O2 through A such that MT is perpendicular to O2M (two choices), and Z the second intersection of line MT with that circle. Prove that M is the midpoint of XY, that M is the midpoint of TZ, and that XY = TZ.
