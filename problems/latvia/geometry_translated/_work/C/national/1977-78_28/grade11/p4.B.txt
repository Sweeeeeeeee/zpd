Take a segment AB. Let C be the point with directed angle (AB, AC) = 60 degrees and directed angle (BA, BC) = -60 degrees (an equilateral triangle on the side fixed by the sign convention; the orientation relative to the drawing is therefore fixed by these signs). Let M be the midpoint of AB. Let D be the intersection of the line through A at directed angle -60 degrees from AB with the line through M at directed angle 60 degrees from MA. Let E be the intersection of the line through B at directed angle 60 degrees from BA with the line through M at directed angle -60 degrees from MB. Let P be the intersection of AB and CD, and Q the intersection of AB and CE. Prove that AP = PQ and PQ = QB.
