Take two free points P and O. Let W be any point on line PO. Let A be any point on the circle centre W through P (so circles (W) and (O) through P are tangent at P). Let B be a point on the tangent at A to circle (W) (line through A perpendicular to WA) that also lies on the circle centre O through P (two intersection points possible, either). Let C be the second intersection (other than B) of line AB with the circle centre O through B. Prove that angle(PB,PA) = angle(PA,PC) (directed angles between lines).
