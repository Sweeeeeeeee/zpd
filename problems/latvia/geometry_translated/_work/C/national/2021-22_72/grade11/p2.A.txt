Let A and B be two arbitrary distinct points. M is the midpoint of AB. O is an arbitrary point and D is an arbitrary point on the circle with center O through A. E is the midpoint of OD and F is the midpoint of DB. N is a point common to the circle with center O through M and the circle with center E through F (the two circles meet in two points, so N has two possible positions). C is the reflection of D through the point N (N is the midpoint of DC). Prove that AB = CD.
