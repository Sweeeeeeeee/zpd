Let O and A be arbitrary points, D an arbitrary point on the circle with center O through A, and B another arbitrary point. M is the midpoint of AB, E the midpoint of OD, F the midpoint of OB. N is a point with ON = OM and NE = OF (intersection of the circle centered O through M with the circle centered E of radius OF; two possible positions). C is the reflection of D through the point N (N is the midpoint of DC). Prove that AB = CD.
