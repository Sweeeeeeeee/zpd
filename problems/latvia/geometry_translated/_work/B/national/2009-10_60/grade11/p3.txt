# notes: the two hypotheses are conditions on the triangle, so the figure is built backwards from them. AT = TB puts T on the perpendicular bisector of AB; the line through T perpendicular to AB is then that bisector, so Q (on it, Q != T) is on the bisector too; with BQ perpendicular to AQ, Q is the apex of the right isosceles triangle on AB (either side, mirror images). Tangency of TB is stated by the tangent-chord angle: angle(TB,TA) = angle(QT,QA), and line QT = line QM (M midpoint of AB); eqangle3 + on_bline gives T (two branches, both satisfy it). omega = circle AQT with centre k; P = second meet of AB with omega; C = AQ meet perpendicular to AB at P, so BQ and CP are altitudes. "Acute" is not imposed.
